This site is a **static** portfolio hosted via GitHub Pages (custom domain: `goldleafinteractive.com`).

- Hosting providers (for example GitHub Pages / GitHub) may process ordinary technical request data such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps under their own policies.
- If I add analytics later, this page will be updated. I do not sell personal data.

## 3. Games and online features

Some games published under Gold Leaf Interactive may include optional online features (login, multiplayer, voice chat) in addition to offline/local play.

### 3.1 Epic Games / Epic Online Services

Certain titles may use **Epic Account Services** and **Epic Online Services (EOS)**, which can include:

- Epic account authentication / login
- Online multiplayer connectivity (for example lobbies or sessions)
- Voice chat (for example proximity or push-to-talk)

When you use those features, **Epic Games, Inc.** and its affiliates process account and service data under Epic’s own terms and privacy notices. I do not receive your Epic password. Depending on what a product enables, I may receive identifiers and technical data needed to run the game (for example EOS product/account identifiers, lobby or session identifiers, and voice-channel connection state).

Review Epic’s current Privacy Policy and Epic Online Services documentation on Epic’s websites for Epic’s practices.

### 3.2 Other platforms

Builds on **itch.io**, **Steam**, or other storefronts are also subject to those platforms’ privacy policies and account systems. Downloads, purchases, updates, and community features on those platforms are handled by the platform operator.

If a game enables voice chat:

- Your microphone may transmit audio to other players while the feature is active

- Do not share sensitive personal information over voice chat
