# Current Pursuits

**A.A.S. Digital Gaming & Simulation (Programmers), HCC** — programming, design literacy, technical art touchpoints, Scrum-style milestones, capstones, portfolio polish, **hardware / unconventional-input** experiments, talking systems to non-programmers.

**Gameplay Systems Engineer** first, **Technical Designer** second—**mechanics, loops, tool-friendly data** from first playable through ship. **Hands-on implementation**; **AI-assisted tooling** where it saves time, but **specs, merges, and sign-off stay mine.**

---

## Skills & Expertise

**Engines & implementation** — Day-to-day depth in **Unity (C#)** and **Unreal (C++ / Blueprints)**: gameplay systems, UI/session flow, tuning data, and builds that actually leave the editor (**WebGL**, Windows, cabinet-style PC). Exploring **Godot** seriously beside the two main stacks.

**Gameplay & systems thinking** — Comfortable where **design and code meet**: hybrid control schemes, readable fail states, escalating sessions, and progression that stays fair as speed or pressure rises. **Management / sim-style** rhythms (nights, economies, central “director” behaviors) and **data-first** workflows (Scriptables, Data Tables, weighted spawns) keep iteration cheap across design and programming.

**Procedural content, done responsibly** — Practical **PCG** with **authored guardrails**—chunked levels, tiered obstacle pools, grapple runs and house layouts that vary without turning into random noise. Balanced with **playability first**, especially on **browser builds** and quirky hardware.

**Unusual inputs & polish paths** — Built **breath hardware** through microcontroller **serial**, background ingest, filtering, and **mic/keyboard fallbacks** when demoing without the rig. **Arcade / gamepad-first** treatment on Unreal shipping work; stylized **CRT** and **shader-driven feedback** when the look matters, with **performance fallbacks** where WebGL or weak GPUs need breathing room.

**How I work** — I ship on **itch** and/or **Steam** as each project dictates. **Short integration maps**—“what this is / where it lives”—mean handoffs don’t hinge only on tacit knowledge. **AI-assisted tooling** helps with drafts and repetition; **I still finalize specs**, **reviews**, **merges**, and **playtests**, and I keep **light notes or devlogs** so milestones, pivots, and major decisions stay **traceable** as the project evolves.
