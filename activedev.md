# Current Pursuits

Recently completed my **A.A.S. in Digital Gaming & Simulation (for Programmers)** — game programming foundations, design literacy, technical art touchpoints, generalist game engine implementation, Scrum-style milestones, capstones, portfolio polishing, **hardware / unconventional-input** experiments, leading & communicating with teams of varying roles (designers, programmers, 2D & 3D artists, writers, etc.)

I'm a **Gameplay Engineer** first, **Technical Game Designer** second— I work on **mechanics, loops, and designer friendly, data-driven features**, taking game concepts from ideas to shippable products. **Hands-on implementation** in game engines; **AI-assisted tooling** where and when it saves time, of course with strict reviewing, analysis, and playtesting before sign-offs.

---

## Skills & Expertise

**Engines & implementation** — Day-to-day depth in **Unity (C#)** and **Unreal (C++ / Blueprints)**: gameplay systems, UI/session flow, tuning data, and packaging builds (**WebGL**, Windows). Planning to explore **Godot** seriously besides those two main stacks that I have learned with thus far.

**Gameplay & systems thinking** — Comfortable where **design and code meet**: hybrid control schemes, readable fail states, tuning escalating difficulties, and game progression that stays fair as speed or pressure rises. **Management / sim-style** rhythms (nights, economies, central “director” behaviors) and **data-first** workflows (Scriptables, Data Tables, weighted spawns) keep iteration cheap across design and programming, and makes designing/tweaking parameters and details in-engine much easier.

**Procedural Content** — Practical **PCG** with **authored guardrails** via data-driven designing—chunked levels, tiered obstacle pools, physics mechanics and level layouts that can vary without turning into random noise. Extensive playtests must often be done with these systems to find comfortable progression ramps and tiering.

**Unusual inputs & polish paths** — Built custom **breath input hardware** through a microcontroller **serial** port-> Unity game connection, with background ingest, filtering, and **mic/keyboard fallbacks** when demoing without the rig. **Arcade / gamepad-first** work on Unreal shipping work; stylized **CRT** and **shader-driven feedback** visual game identity, providing **performance fallbacks** where WebGL or weak GPUs need breathing room, and building settings to configure microphones, serial COM ports, & more.

**How I work** — I currently ship on **itch** and/or **Steam**. I'm comfortable learning new softwares and interfaces, and I like to incorporate the latest tech into my workflow when possible. **AI-assisted tooling** helps with organizing documents, drafts, repetition, & more; **I still finalize specs**, **reviews**, **merges**, and **playtests**, and I keep **notes or devlogs, as well as individual session logs** so that milestones, pivots, and major decisions or original intent stay **traceable** as the project evolves.
