# Current Pursuits

Recently completed my **A.A.S. in Digital Gaming & Simulation (Programmers)** — programming foundations, design literacy, technical art, Scrum milestones, applied game programming, level design, capstones, **hardware/alt-input** experiments, generalist foundations of game development, and much more.

I am a **Gameplay Engineer** first, **Technical Designer** second — I build **mechanics, loops, and data-driven features** from concept to ship. My daily work usually consists of **hands-on engine work** with **AI-assisted tooling** under strict review and playtesting to ensure feature completion as intended.

---

## Skills & Expertise

**Engines & implementation** — Day-to-day depth in **Unity (C#)** and **Unreal (C++ / Blueprints)**: gameplay systems, UI/session flow, tuning data, and packaging builds (**WebGL**, Windows). Planning to explore **Godot** seriously besides those two main stacks that I have learned with thus far.

**Gameplay & systems thinking** — Comfortable where **design and code meet**: hybrid control schemes, readable fail states, tuning escalating difficulties, and game progression that stays fair as speed or pressure rises. **Management / sim-style** rhythms (nights, economies, central "director" behaviors) and **data-first** workflows (Scriptables, Data Tables, weighted spawns) can keep iteration cheap across design and programming, and makes designing/tweaking parameters and details in-engine much easier.

**Procedural Content** — Practical **PCG** with **authored guardrails** via data-driven designing—chunked levels and tiered obstacle pools direct the PCG, also build procedural effects, mechanics and level layouts that can vary without turning into random noise, directed variation. Extensive playtests must often be done with these systems to find comfortable progression ramps and tiering.

**Unusual inputs & polish paths** — Built custom **breath input hardware** through a microcontroller **serial** port-> Unity game connection, with background ingest, filtering, and **mic/keyboard fallbacks** when demoing without the rig. **Arcade / gamepad-first** work on Unreal shipping work; stylized **CRT** and **shader-driven feedback** visual game identity, providing **performance fallbacks** where WebGL or weak GPUs need breathing room, and building settings to configure microphones, serial COM ports, & more.

**How I work** — I currently ship on **itch** and/or **Steam**. I'm comfortable learning new softwares and interfaces, and I like to incorporate the latest tech into my workflow when possible. **AI-assisted tooling** helps with organizing documents, drafts, repetition, & more; **I still finalize specs**, **reviews**, **merges**, and **playtests**, and I keep **notes or devlogs, as well as individual session logs** so that milestones, pivots, and major decisions or original intent stay **traceable** as the project evolves.
